Frequently Asked Questions

How fresh will my roasted coffee beans be?

We roast daily in micro-batches. Your order is dispatched within 24 to 48 hours of roasting, reaching you during peak degassing freshness (days 4 through 28 post-roast).

Can I have my beans ground for my specific coffee maker?

Yes! You can choose between Whole Bean, Espresso, and Filter / V60 upon ordering. If you have custom requirements (like AeroPress fine or Cold Brew coarse), simply leave a note during checkout.

Do you provide coffee machine equipment and beans for corporate offices?

Absolutely. We provide end-to-end commercial solutions including Synesso espresso machinery, Mahlkönig commercial grinders, and weekly nitrogen-flushed bean deliveries across India.
