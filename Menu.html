09 — The Science of Extraction

Precision Down to the Milligram

Coffee is 98.5% water and 1.5% dissolved solubles. We engineer fluid dynamics, water minerality, and thermal stability to eliminate astringency.

125 PPM

Mineral-Balanced Water

Tap water is the silent killer of coffee flavor. We strip water via Reverse Osmosis and inject Magnesium (Mg²⁺) to bind delicate fruit notes, and Calcium (Ca²⁺) for creamy body balance.

2:1 Mg/Ca Ratio 7.2 Neutral pH RO Filtration
±0.1 °C

Multi-Boiler Thermal Lock

Our Synesso MVP Hydra machines feature individual group saturated boilers. Zero temperature drift between consecutive extractions prevents bitter scorching of light roasts.

Hydra PID Tech Saturated Groups Dual Pumps
99.2 %

Unimodal Particle Grinding

Boulders cause watery under-extraction while fines cause astringent bitterness. Our 98mm German cast steel EK43 burrs produce laser-uniform grind particles.

98mm Steel Burrs Zero Retention Laser Calibrated
14 Days

Nitrogen Degas Protocol

Fresh-roasted beans release volatile CO2 gas that disrupts extraction. We rest every single roast batch in nitrogen-flushed vaults until cellular structure reaches maximum aromatics.

Optimal Degas Nitrogen Valve Aroma Peak
