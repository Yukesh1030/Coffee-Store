Sensory Experience

Upon grinding, an intense bouquet of crushed white jasmine, bergamot oil, and elderflower fills the air. The hot extraction starts with lively acidity reminiscent of blood orange and lemon curd, before cooling into a velvety cup saturated with wildflower honey and dried peach.

The Yirgacheffe Region

Located in the Gedeo Zone of southern Ethiopia at altitudes exceeding 1,950 meters. The cool mountain nights allow coffee cherries to mature slowly, concentrating natural fructose and organic phosphoric acids. Grown primarily by smallholders on ancestral forest garden plots.

Recommended V60 Pour Over Recipe

  • Dose: 15g medium-fine ground coffee
  • Water: 250g soft filtered water at 93°C
  • Ratio: 1:16.6
  • Bloom: 45g water, gently swirl, wait 45 seconds
  • Total Brew Time: 2 minutes 45 seconds
